## 🚀 What Is Codebone?

Codebone is a friendly desktop app for your Mac that creates a living map of your codebase. It watches your project as you work, understands how all the pieces connect, and shares that understanding with AI coding assistants like Claude Code, Cursor, or VS Code. Think of it as a GPS for your code—it shows the AI where everything leads.

No more vague answers from AI about your project. Codebone gives AI tools a clear, up-to-date picture of your architecture so they can give you precise, context-aware suggestions.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "The app won't open" or "Cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software"
This happens because Codebone is not from the Mac App Store. To fix it:
1. Go to **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security**
2. Scroll down to **Security**
3. Click **Open Anyway** next to the Codebone message
4. Confirm with **Open**

### "My AI assistant says it can't connect to Codebone"
- Make sure Codebone is still running in the background (check the menu bar icon)
- Restart both Codebone and your AI tool
- Check that you followed the connection steps in the Codebone app

### "The knowledge graph looks incomplete"
- Give Codebone a minute to finish scanning larger projects
- Make sure your project folder doesn't contain ignored build folders like `node_modules` or `build` (Codebone automatically skips these)

### "I have an older Mac"
Codebone needs macOS 13 or newer. If you're on an older version, consider updating your Mac or reach out for help.
